Yutong Song

Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science · University of California, Irvine

I am a fourth-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of California, Irvine, where I work in the Health SciTech Group. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Amir M. Rahmani and Prof. Nikil Dutt. I expect to graduate in 2026 and live in Irvine with my cat Hula.

My research lies at the intersection of large language models, personalization, and health AI. I build language agents that adapt to individual users and complex real-world contexts, with recent work in personalized text generation, dementia dialogue simulation, medical speech processing, and knowledge-grounded reasoning.

My broader interests include LLM post-training, user modeling, reinforcement learning, human preference alignment, agentic RAG, speech language models, spoken dialogue systems, and automatic speech recognition.

I am currently interested in research and applied scientist opportunities in LLM post-training, personalization, health AI, and agentic systems.
