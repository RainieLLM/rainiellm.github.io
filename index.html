Selected Publications 🚀

Google Scholar

EMNLP 2026

Do Implicit Personalization and Explicit Styles Conflict? PsPLUG: A Lightweight Plug-in for Balancing Personalization and Style in Customized LLMs

Yutong Song, Jiang Wu, Shaofan Yuan, Chengze Shen, Jian Wang, Yu Wang, Nikil Dutt, Amir M. Rahmani

PsPLUG learns a user-specific residual after accounting for the requested style, preserving implicit user preferences while offering precise control over personalization strength and style adherence.

Paper

EMNLP 2026 Findings

CARD: Cluster-level Adaptation with Reward-guided Decoding for Personalized Text Generation

Yutong Song, Jiang Wu, Weijia Zhang, Chengze Shen, Shaofan Yuan, Weitao Lu, Jian Wang, Yu Wang, Nikil Dutt, Amir M. Rahmani

CARD is a hierarchical personalization text generation framework that combines group-level adaptation with user-specific decoding to generate text better aligned with individual preferences.

PaperSlides

EMNLP 2026

AP-GRPO: Anchor-Gated Phonetic Alignment with Policy Optimization for Pathological Speech Reconstruction

Pengfei Zhang, Hoang H. Nguyen, Yutong Song, Wenjun Huang, Tahmid Imtiaz Imu, Henry Peng Zou, Jiang Wu, Honghui Xu, Amir M. Rahmani

AP-GRPO improves pathological speech reconstruction with anchor-gated and inter-anchor phonetic rewards that preserve reliable speech evidence while recovering corrupted content.

Paper

ACL 2026

DemMA: Dementia Multi-Turn Dialogue Agent with Expert-Guided Reasoning and Action Simulation

Yutong Song, Jiang Wu, Kazi Sharif, Honghui Xu, Nikil Dutt, Amir Rahmani

DemMA is an expert-guided dementia dialogue agent that jointly models cognitive impairment, emotional dynamics, and nonverbal behaviors for high-fidelity multi-turn patient simulation.

PaperPosterCode

ICASSP 2026

MedSpeak: A Knowledge Graph-Aided ASR Error Correction Framework for Spoken Medical QA

Yutong Song, Shiva Shrestha, Chenhan Lyu, Elahe Khatibi, Pengfei Zhang, Honghui Xu, Nikil Dutt, Amir Rahmani

MedSpeak uses semantic relationships and phonetic information from a medical knowledge graph, together with LLM reasoning, to correct noisy ASR transcripts and improve spoken medical question answering.
